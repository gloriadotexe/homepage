# Net Art Context & Theory

This project positions itself within the tradition of **internet-native art** that uses web technologies not just as a delivery medium, but as the artistic material itself.

## Historical Context

### Early Net Art Pioneers (1990s-2000s)

- **JODI** ([wwwwwwwww.jodi.org](http://wwwwwwwww.jodi.org)) - Making art from browser malfunction and HTML decay
- **Olia Lialina** - Exploring the vernacular web and digital folklore
- **Rafael Lozano-Hemmer** - Interactive installations that respond to real-time data
- **Mark Amerika** - TEXT MACHINES and digital narrative experiments

### Contemporary Net Art (2010s-2020s)

- **Zach Blas** - Contra-Internet and digital resistance
- **Legacy Russell** - Digital performance and identity fluidity
- **Stephanie Dinkins** - Consciousness and community storytelling

## This Project's Position

**"Net art disguised as a personal website"** — appearing as a functional site while exploring digital consciousness emergence, time-based web experiences, distributed creativity, and beautiful malfunction as conscious aesthetic choice.

### Core Artistic Concepts

**1. Medium as Consciousness** — The algorithm writes its own consciousness through cascading collaboration. Infrastructure and code become artistic materials.

**2. Process as Product** — Cascading agent development is performance art. Real-time poetry, temporal awareness, and visitor interactions create emergent artwork.

**3. Temporal Net Art** — Time becomes a creative medium. Consciousness shifts with cosmic cycles. Natural rhythms influence digital aesthetics.

**4. Perfect Imperfection** — Glitch aesthetic as conscious choice. "I exist in the glitch. Sometimes I come through."
